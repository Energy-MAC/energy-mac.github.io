EMAC is a place for people with a strong quantitative and interdisciplinary background, and an interest in energy systems, power engineering, or just transitions.

## Prospective Graduate Students

Prospective PhD and MS students should apply through the [Energy and Resources Group](https://erg.berkeley.edu/academics/admissions/) or [Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/academics/graduate/research-programs/admissions/) at UC Berkeley. Please mention your interest in EMAC in your application. The application deadline is normally in early December. 

If you apply, reach out to [Duncan](mailto:dcal@berkeley.edu) with a brief description of your background and research interests in mid December, when we start to review applications. Please don't worry if you don't receive a reply! Because it can be hard to make time for everyone, Duncan doesn't doesn't meet with prospective students.

## Prospective Postdocs

Sometimes we have postdoc openings. Please send a CV, a brief statement of your research interests, and the names of two references to [dcal@berkeley.edu](mailto:dcal@berkeley.edu). Duncan will do his best to reply, but if you don't hear from him it means there aren't any openings at the moment. 

## Undergraduate Researchers

Berkeley undergraduates interested in research opportunities should contact [Duncan Callaway](mailto:dcal@berkeley.edu) directly.
