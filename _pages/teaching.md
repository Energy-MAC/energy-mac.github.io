## Current Courses

### ERG 131 / ESPM 134: Renewable Energy and the Grid
An introduction to the engineering and economics of renewable energy integration in electric power systems. Topics include power systems fundamentals, electricity markets, storage, demand response, and policy.

### ERG 230: Energy and Society
Graduate seminar examining the relationship between energy systems and society, with attention to equity, environment, and political economy.
