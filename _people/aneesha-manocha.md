Aneesha Manocha (she/her) is particularly interested in developing optimization methods and macro-energy system models to plan decarbonized energy futures. More specifically, she aims to research how to prioritize historical environmental injustices in modeling frameworks. Aneesha was a Kanders Churchill Scholar at the University of Cambridge and completed her undergraduate degree at Princeton University  in Electrical and Computer Engineering.
